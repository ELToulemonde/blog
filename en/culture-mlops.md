## Treating ML like software

Between high user expectations and production challenges, building a Machine Learning product often feels like an emotional roller coaster. Our MLOps approach brings together the best of craftsmanship, emergent architecture, Ops, agility, and Lean.

The book combines ideas, principles, and field experience around four goals:

- reaching production without getting lost in processes;
- making systems resilient, reproducible, and repairable;
- accelerating feedback loops;
- visualising work and quality to improve practices.

## The authors

Sofia Calcagno and Emmanuel-Lin Toulemonde support teams designing, industrialising, and operating Machine Learning products. This book brings together lessons from their field experience.
